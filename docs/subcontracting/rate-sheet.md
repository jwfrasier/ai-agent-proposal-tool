**Fixed-price workstream (default).** You hand us a bounded scope: a feature, a pipeline, a dashboard, a modernization sprint. We return a fixed price, a named team, and milestone dates within five business days. Payment on milestone acceptance. This is how most of our work is priced and it keeps your margin predictable.

**Hourly, when scope is open.** Labor-hour at the rates below, monthly invoicing, weekly burn report against a not-to-exceed you set.

## Set-aside math

Frasier Digital is a small business. On a total small-business set-aside our share is similarly situated under 13 CFR 125.6 and does not count against the 50 percent you must retain. On 8(a), WOSB, SDVOSB, or HUBZone set-asides it does count, and we scope accordingly, typically a single workstream at 20 to 30 percent of contract value.
