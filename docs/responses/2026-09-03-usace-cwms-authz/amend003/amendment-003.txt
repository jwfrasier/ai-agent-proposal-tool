III. SCHEDULE OF SUPPLIES/SERVICES:

The contractor will provide support for software enhancements to the CWMS, focusing on the
database and API components. This includes improving the authorization web UI, implementing
the new authorization system within the CWBI cloud environment, conducting load testing, and
performing ongoing maintenance and improvements based on user feedback.

Period of Performance:
The period of performance is 360 calendar days from date of contract award.
Place of Performance:
The Offeror’s facility. See the PWS for additional information.
