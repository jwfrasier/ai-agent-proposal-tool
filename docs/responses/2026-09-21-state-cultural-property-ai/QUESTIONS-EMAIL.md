Ms. DiAngelo,

Frasier Digital LLC respectfully submits the following questions regarding RFQ 19AQMM26Q0468, Cultural Property AI Prototype.

**1. Magnitude of effort.** To help quoters propose a level of effort and labor mix aligned with the Government's expectations for a nine-month proof-of-concept, is the Government able to share the anticipated price magnitude or funding range for this requirement?

**2. Place of performance.** The RFQ does not identify a place of performance. Will the kickoff meeting (SOW Task 1), stakeholder consultations (Task 2), simulated field testing (Task 6), and final briefing (Task 8) be conducted virtually, or does the Government expect in-person attendance in the Washington, DC area or at law enforcement facilities? If in person, approximately how many on-site events should quoters plan for?

**3. Secure Software Development Attestation Form (Attachment 3) for software to be developed under the order.** The prototype application does not yet exist; it will be developed under this purchase order. Should quoters submit a company-wide attestation covering the software producer's development practices, listing the prototype as the product to which the attestation will apply, and then re-submit a product-specific attestation upon delivery? Or does the Government consider the attestation not applicable at quote submission for software that has not yet been produced?

**4. Applicability of clause 652.239-71 to a standalone prototype.** The SOW requires a standalone system that does not connect to Department networks and does not process or store Department information (SOW Task 4). Clause 652.239-71 applies to systems "connected to a Department of State network or operated by the Contractor for DOS," and paragraph (d) requires written proof of NIST SP 800-37 accreditation within six months of award. Please confirm whether paragraphs (c) and (d) of 652.239-71 apply to this prototype and, if so, whether the six-month accreditation deliverable is intended to fall within the nine-month period of performance.

**5. Hosting model for the "standalone application."** Will the Government accept a prototype delivered as a contractor-hosted web application in a commercial cloud environment (with no connection to Government networks) for simulated field testing, or does "standalone" require an application that runs entirely on the tester's device without any hosted component?

**6. CLIN 002 quantity.** CLIN 002 (Monthly Progress Reports) shows a quantity of 12, while the SOW states a nine-month period of performance. Should quoters price nine monthly reports, or twelve?

**7. Data rights.** SOW Section 10.2 provides Government Purpose Rights under FAR 52.227-14 Alternate IV and expressly preserves the Contractor's right to commercialize the prototype and models. Clause H-020 (Safeguarding Information) states that all documents and records generated under the contract become the exclusive property of the Government. Please confirm that SOW Section 10 governs rights in the prototype, models, and associated data and software delivered under this order.

**8. Government facilitation of stakeholder and tester access.** Will the Government (CHC/CHCC) facilitate introductions to, and participation by, the Cultural Property Experts On Call program, U.S. law enforcement and customs personnel, and CHCC member agencies for the stakeholder consultations in Task 2 and the simulated field testing in Task 6, or is the Contractor expected to secure that participation independently?

**9. 3D models.** SOW Section 3 references training data "including images, 3D models, and related metadata." Is the inclusion of 3D model data a requirement of the prototype's training set, or is image-based identification sufficient for the proof-of-concept?

**10. Third-party data costs.** SOW Task 3 directs the Contractor to prefer Creative Commons 0 data and to negotiate other data-sharing arrangements "at no additional cost." Please confirm that licensing fees for any non-free datasets, if the Contractor elects to use them, must be included in the firm-fixed price and will not be separately reimbursed.

**11. Acquisition procedures.** RFQ Section 1.1 references "the procedures in Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 13.2." Please confirm that this order will be placed under the simplified acquisition procedures of FAR Part 13 (Subpart 13.3), rather than the micro-purchase procedures of Subpart 13.2.

Thank you for your consideration. We look forward to the Government's responses.
