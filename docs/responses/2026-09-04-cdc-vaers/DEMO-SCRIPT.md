# DEMO-SCRIPT.md — the script the prototype is built toward

This is the spec. Every scene names who is acting, what they do, the one thing
that must land, which scored PRS rows it hits, and the build guarantees that
keep it true. The Tab 2-2 "suggested walkthrough" is a compressed version of
this script; `e2e.js` and `a11y.js` are its enforcement; the pre-send checklist
in `prototype/DEPLOY.md` is its dress rehearsal.

Rule of construction: **each scene proves exactly one claim.** If a feature
doesn't land inside a scene, it either gets a scene or gets cut from Tab 2-2.

Total run time: ~12 minutes. Scenes 2 and 3 are the ones evaluators grade
hardest (PRS#1 is the first row of their matrix); nothing may dilute them.

---

**Scene 0 (standing):** the footer's "For RFQ evaluators" page carries this
script in-app, with one-click sample scenarios (parent case, pharmacist case)
that land directly on the review step — no evaluator ever has to hand-type a
report to see Scenes 4-6.

## Act I — Arrival (evaluator as themselves, ~1 min)

### Scene 1: "This is real, and it's honest"
- **Who:** CDC evaluator, desktop, first click on the Tab 2-2 link.
- **Action:** Open the URL. Read the page. Click nothing yet.
- **Must land:** A production-credible federal site loads instantly; the purple
  demonstration banner declares synthetic data before anyone asks; the three
  PRS#3 access paths (report, FAQs, data) are unmissable, with "start a report"
  visually primary.
- **Scores:** PRS#3 (landing/nav), PRS#4 (speed), understanding criterion.
- **Build guarantees:**
  - First paint under 1s uncached; no layout shift.
  - Demo banner present on every page; never styled like product chrome.
  - Report path is the single dominant action; FAQ and data are subordinate.

## Act II — The parent (public path, phone-in-hand mindset, ~4 min)

### Scene 2: "It speaks my language" — now opens with the story
- **New beat:** before touching a single field, the parent can write what
  happened in their own words; the prototype proposes form answers drawn only
  from that story, each reviewed and confirmed, then tagged "AI-suggested ·
  verify" on the form. Burden reduction shown at its most literal.
- **Payoff beat (added 8/20):** on Apply, an in-place receipt confirms where
  the answers went ("7 answers applied to the steps ahead — About the vaccine
  (2) · What happened after the vaccine (4)…"), the completeness meter jumps
  (~40% from one paragraph), and purple "N AI" chips sit on each section in
  the completeness panel until the user verifies those fields. Never let the
  demo imply the story vanished — the applied answers are visible from the
  moment of application. (Regression: `narrative-e2e.js`, 17 assertions.)

- **Who:** Persona: parent, 9pm, worried about a child's fever after a flu shot.
- **Action:** Start a report. Choose "patient, parent, or member of the
  public." Advance one step.
- **Must land:** Every question reads like a person, not a regulation:
  "Tell us what happened," not "Describe the adverse event." The
  plain/clinical toggle proves the substitution is engineered (1.6.1), not
  copywritten once.
- **Scores:** PRS#1 (public path), understanding + innovation criteria.
- **Build guarantees:**
  - Public path defaults to plain language; toggle flips wording live with no
    state loss.
  - No clinical vocabulary on the public path, ever.

### Scene 3: "It helps me while I type"
- **Who:** Same parent, mid-form.
- **Action:** Open a "Why we ask" tooltip; skip required fields and hit
  Continue to trigger validation; watch the completeness meter move; ask the
  assistant "where is the lot number?"
- **Must land:** Guidance arrives in context (tooltip, assist rail, error
  recovery that never scolds), and the meter visibly climbs toward
  submit-ready: data quality made tangible (the ≥90% completeness metric CDC
  will measure, PRS#5, shown live to the person who controls it).
- **Scores:** PRS#5, intelligent-assistance objective, innovation criterion.
- **Build guarantees:**
  - Error summary + inline messages; focus moves to the summary; messages say
    what to do, never blame.
  - Meter updates on every answer; reaching 100% is visibly rewarded.
  - Assistant answers are generated by live AI (same-origin endpoint; only the
    typed question or draft text is sent, never form answers) and every answer
    is labeled "AI answer" or "scripted answer."
  - The scripted knowledge base is an automatic fallback: the scene completes
    identically with the AI endpoint unreachable.
  - On narrative fields with a draft, the "check my description" coach returns
    specific missing-detail prompts (the PRS#5 completeness metric, actively
    coached rather than just measured).
  - Safety-critical inputs never reach the generative model: a classifier
    stage routes emergencies, medical-advice requests, causality questions,
    shared personal information, and injection attempts to fixed approved
    responses. The 911 message is never model-generated.

### Scene 4: "Documents are easy, and the rules are visible"
- **Who:** Same parent, at Supporting documents.
- **Action:** Attach a PDF (accepted). Attempt a phone photo (rejected with the
  Phase 2 explanation). Add a free-text note.
- **Must land:** Phase 1 restriction enforced politely and the Phase 2 design
  intent stated in the rejection itself (PRS#11's two halves in one moment).
- **Scores:** PRS#9, PRS#11.
- **Build guarantees:**
  - Accept list and rejection copy match the PWS wording (medical records and
    vaccine documents).
  - Free-text box present; nothing actually uploads; the widget says so.

### Scene 5: "I can see exactly what I sent"
- **Who:** Same parent, finishing.
- **Action:** Review answers, submit, read the confirmation, open and download
  the structured record. Dismiss (or complete) the popup survey.
- **Must land:** Submission produces a legible, field-mapped VAERS-compatible
  record on screen: integration shown as data, not claimed in prose. The
  post-submission survey appears without trapping anyone.
- **Scores:** PRS#6, PRS#7 (survey one of two), PRS#4 (elapsed-time line).
- **Build guarantees:**
  - Review shows only answered fields, grouped by section, edit-by-going-back.
  - Record viewer + JSON download; meta block states synthetic/demo status.
  - Record field names match the published VAERS data dictionary (VAERSDATA /
    VAERSVAX), outcomes as Y-flags, NUMDAYS computed, identifiers split into a
    confidential section mirroring the public-release de-identification.
  - Survey is dismissible, keyboard-trapped while open, focus returns on close.

## Act III — The pharmacist (provider path, ~2 min)

### Scene 6: "Eleven fewer questions" (the PRS#1 centerpiece)
- **Who:** Persona: pharmacist between customers; gave a dose from an expired
  vial, patient is fine.
- **Action:** Start over as healthcare professional. Choose "administration
  error with no adverse event." Walk the shortened form. At review, read the
  auto-suggested documents.
- **Must land:** The form visibly contracts the instant the error path is
  chosen: stepper loses its adverse-event steps and a note states how many
  questions were removed. Then the suggestion tool reads the submission
  (expired vial → storage log + administration record) and proves PRS#10 with
  the pharmacist's own inputs.
- **Scores:** PRS#1 (the named scenario), PRS#10, burden-reduction objective.
- **Build guarantees:**
  - Zero AE-related fields reachable on this path, including via Back.
  - Questions-removed count computed from the schema, never hard-coded.
  - Suggestions derive from entered answers; changing answers changes them.
  - Clinical language automatic on the provider path.

## Act IV — The program staffer (admin, ~2 min)

### Scene 7: "Change it yourself, watch it change"
- **Who:** Persona: CDC program staffer who needs to reword a tooltip today,
  not file a dev ticket.
- **Action:** Open Admin. Edit the lot-number tooltip and one FAQ answer.
  Watch the live preview. Return to the form and see the edit live. Reset all.
- **Must land:** PRS#8's acceptance test performed literally: a content update
  completed with no developer, plus proof it round-trips into the real form
  (the preview renders the production Field component, and says so).
- **Scores:** PRS#8, scalability criterion (config-driven architecture made
  visible).
- **Build guarantees:**
  - Edit visibly marked as applied; Reset restores defaults completely.
  - Preview is the production component, not a facsimile.
  - Interface edits work, not just content: hide an optional field, reword a
    choice option, add an option — all live, no developer.

## Act V — The skeptic (adversarial pass, ~3 min)

### Scene 8: "Try to break it"
- **Who:** The evaluator turning hostile: phone out, mouse unplugged.
- **Action:** Re-run Scene 6 on a phone. Tab through a full step
  keyboard-only. Close the laptop mid-report, reopen, resume. Read the FAQ
  page; open the footer navigation survey (survey two of two).
- **Must land:** Nothing degrades: the phone gets the same flows plus an
  inline assist panel, keyboard focus is always visible, the half-finished
  report is still there, and the second survey exists where PWS 1.5 says.
- **Scores:** PRS#2, PRS#7, PRS#14, feasibility criterion.
- **Build guarantees:**
  - Every scene in this script completes on a 390px viewport.
  - `a11y.js` (WCAG 2.1 AA + 508) passes on all pages and demo states.
  - Save/resume survives reload; Start over fully clears.

## Change control

A code change is demo-ready only if: (1) it serves a scene's "must land" or a
build guarantee; (2) `e2e.js` and `a11y.js` still pass; (3) any new
demo-critical behavior gets its beat added here in the same commit. The script
and the app move together, or not at all.
