## Top-line assessment (honest version)

**Frasier Digital cannot submit a standard MAS offer today, and the workaround path is gated on a federal agency sponsor.**

1. **Springboard is the required path as the facts stand.** SAM Core Data shows entity start date **Jan 6, 2026**. The solicitation requires "a minimum of two (2) years of corporate experience … unless submitting under Startup Springboard" (SCP-FSS-001 (j) Factor One (A)) and "annual financial statements for the previous two-years" (SCP-FSS-001 (j)(1)(v)). The LLC has neither.
2. **Springboard is no longer open-door.** "As of Refresh 31 publication, Startup Springboard participation is limited to FASt Lane participants only" (SCP-FSS-001 (a)), and FASt Lane now requires **agency sponsorship** — a written request from a federal ordering activity ([FASt Lane](https://www.gsa.gov/technology/it-contract-vehicles-and-purchasing-programs/multiple-award-schedule-it/fast-lane-making-it-easier); [Winvale summary](https://info.winvale.com/blog/everything-need-know-gsas-fast-lane-program)). Frasier has no such sponsor today.
3. **The past-performance base is sufficient *only* under Springboard.** Refresh 32 moved the "substitute work performed by affiliates, predecessor companies, or key personnel" allowance for both Past Performance references and Relevant Project Experience to **"Applicable to Startup Springboard Only"** (SCP-FSS-001 (j)(2) Factors Two and Four). Outside Springboard, all three required references and the project narrative(s) must be work the *company* performed — and Frasier's company-performed record is Region 4 (entity attribution unresolved, see below) plus one $1,000 engagement.
4. **The single biggest unknown is an entity question, not a GSA question:** which legal entity performed the ~$2M Region 4 engagement (Jul 2024–Oct 2025)? SAM says the LLC started Jan 6, 2026 — after Region 4 ended. If the work ran through a predecessor (sole proprietorship or prior entity) with demonstrable continuity, there is an argument for corporate experience and company past performance; if it was Joseph's personal 1099 work with no business continuity, Frasier's company-performed record starts in 2026. **This one fact decides the path.** UNVERIFIED — only Joseph can resolve it.

(SCP-FSS-001 (j)(2) Factor One; [source](https://sws.gsa.gov/sws-search/generateClauseBodyPdf.do?method=generateClauseBody&solNo=NDdRU01EMjBSMDAwMQ%3D%3D&refreshNo=32&clauseNo=SCP-FSS-001&clauseType=O&sequenceNo=0))

### 2. Startup Springboard — what it substitutes and what it now requires

**GSA demands:** For offerors with <2 years corporate experience, Springboard substitutes:
- *For corporate experience:* "additional information that demonstrates their ability to manage a company and provide the products/services described under this offer (i.e. information on the professional management and project experience of company executives and key personnel)" (SCP-FSS-001 Factor One).
- *For financials:* "financial documentation that demonstrates the Offeror's financial responsibility" in lieu of 2-year statements; the [Springboard page](https://www.gsa.gov/sell-to-government/step-1-learn-about-government-contracting/how-to-access-contract-opportunities/help-with-mas-contracts-to-sell-to-government/roadmap-to-get-a-mas-contract/startup-springboard) lists acceptable substitutes: bank references, SBA Certificate of Competency, asset/liability documentation, line of credit, irrevocable letter of credit, interim financial statements, signed investor/VC agreements.
- *For past performance and project experience:* Springboard-only substitution of "relevant work performed by affiliates, predecessor companies, or key personnel," with the narrative explaining "how the entity or personnel will be meaningfully involved in performing the MAS contract" (SCP-FSS-001 Factors Two & Four, Refresh 32).

**Key-personnel string attached:** "Proposed and accepted key personnel are expected to remain dedicated to the contract. The resumes of the initially proposed key personnel will become the minimum requirement for qualifications for the rest of the total contract term," and substitutions need CO approval ([Springboard page](https://www.gsa.gov/sell-to-government/step-1-learn-about-government-contracting/how-to-access-contract-opportunities/help-with-mas-contracts-to-sell-to-government/roadmap-to-get-a-mas-contract/startup-springboard)). With an all-1099 bench, whoever is named key personnel (realistically Joseph, possibly 1–2 senior consultants) sets a floor Frasier must staff to for up to 20 years.

**Eligibility restriction:** "Startup Springboard is available exclusively to offers within the Information Technology category who are participating in a FASt-lane initiative and are agency-sponsored" ([Springboard page](https://www.gsa.gov/sell-to-government/step-1-learn-about-government-contracting/how-to-access-contract-opportunities/help-with-mas-contracts-to-sell-to-government/roadmap-to-get-a-mas-contract/startup-springboard); confirmed in SCP-FSS-001 (a)). The Refresh 33 advance notice keeps "Startup Springboard … limited to contractors approved for FASt Lane" but expands FASt Lane consideration to offerors who "demonstrate a validated federal requirement supported by agency sponsorship **or documented customer demand**" ([iQuasar Refresh 33 analysis](https://iquasar.com/blog/gsa-just-dropped-the-refresh-33-advanced-notice-here-is-why-this-one-is-different/)) — the "documented customer demand" phrase, if it survives into the September 2026 refresh, may be an opening worth watching. UNVERIFIED until Refresh 33 publishes.

**Frasier has:** IT-category offering (54151S) ✓; strong key-personnel resume (Joseph: Region 4 PM+dev, PwC enterprise AI, LDOE C++ statewide platform, 11+ yrs) ✓; **no agency sponsor** ✗.

**Verdict: DECISION-NEEDED.** Springboard fits Frasier's substance perfectly; the gate is purely the sponsorship letter. Candidate sponsors are agencies already in the pipeline that may want to order via eBuy (SSS if Readiness Sim lands; the CDC/NOAA/DoWEA relationships are earlier-stage).

### 4. Past performance — three references + PPQs

**GSA demands (no CPARS case, which is Frasier's):** "a list of three (3) relevant customer references and three (3) completed Past Performance Questionnaires (PPQ)… from three (3) distinct orders and/or contracts," work "similar in scope," "ongoing or completed within three (3) years preceding the date of offer submission." Each reference: customer name, POC (name/phone/email), description of work and offeror's role, dollar value, period of performance. PPQs completed by the customer before submission (or a signed letter committing to complete on CO request). Refresh 32 additions: "GSA may request supporting documentation (e.g., invoices, statements of work, SF 1449, subcontract agreements, etc.) to verify the legitimacy of the relevant customer references" and may reject references from entities the offeror partially/wholly owns. (SCP-FSS-001 (j)(2) Factor Two.)

**What replaced Open Ratings:** nothing third-party — the Open Ratings/Dun & Bradstreet report requirement was dropped years ago; the current regime is exactly the CPARS-or-references+PPQ structure above, with GSA's own PPQ template on the [MAS Roadmap templates page](https://www.gsa.gov/sell-to-government/step-1-learn-about-government-contracting/how-to-access-contract-opportunities/help-with-mas-contracts-to-sell-to-government/roadmap-to-get-a-mas-contract/required-templates-for-a-mas-offer).

Within the 3-year window until **Oct 2028**. ⚠️ Two live issues: (a) the dollar figure given must be what Region 4's books verify — the consistency map (memory: frasier-past-performance) says ~$400k is in already-submitted documents elsewhere and any PPQ figure must be Ethan-confirmed; (b) **Ethan's Region 4 contract ends Aug 31, 2026** — durable contact info must be captured now.

### 6. Pricing support — TDR, not CSP

**GSA demands:** "This solicitation mandates Transactional Data Reporting (TDR) for all SINs" (SCP-FSS-001 (f), citing GSAR 552.238-80 (DEC 2025 deviation)). CSP-1, Most Favored Customer disclosure, and the Price Reductions Clause are gone as of Refresh 31 (Apr 2026) ([Holland & Knight analysis](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/02/goodbye-prc-hello-tdr-breaking-down-gsas-latest-shift-in-refresh-31); [GSA TDR press release, Jan 2026](https://www.gsa.gov/about-us/newsroom/news-releases/gsa-to-expand-efficiency-through-expanded-tdr-01122026); [GSA TDR help](https://www.gsa.gov/sell-to-government/step-1-learn-about-government-contracting/how-to-access-contract-opportunities/help-with-mas-contracts-to-sell-to-government/transactional-data-reporting/help-with-tdr)). At offer time: "You are not required to submit data in support of offered pricing at this time"; GSA "will maximize its use of existing government data… before requesting additional information" (SCP-FSS-001 Section III, FAR 15.4 class deviation RFO-2025-15). Post-award: report transactional line-item data and remit the Industrial Funding Fee (0.75% — standard MAS rate; confirm current rate at award, UNVERIFIED at Refresh 32). Refresh 33 advance notice signals tightened TDR enforcement ([iQuasar](https://iquasar.com/blog/gsa-just-dropped-the-refresh-33-advanced-notice-here-is-why-this-one-is-different/)).

**Frasier has:** No CSP burden to fear (the historically painful disclosure is abolished — good timing for a company with one $1k commercial sale). Needs: completed Services Price Proposal Template with proposed labor categories/rates defensible against market data (GSA compares against CALC+/existing contract data).

**Verdict: READY-able.** This requirement got dramatically easier in 2026. Build rates from recent bid pricing (SSS RS, NOAA, DoWEA rate structures) benchmarked against CALC+ for 54151S equivalents.

### 13. SDB status — does it help?

**In MAS award: no.** MAS evaluation is pass/fail responsibility + technical acceptability + fair-and-reasonable pricing; there is no socioeconomic evaluation preference for getting on Schedule. (SCP-FSS-001 generally; no SDB provision exists in the offer instructions.)

**In eBuy competition: yes, materially, at the buyer's discretion.** Under [FAR 8.405-5](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/8.405-5), ordering activities *may* set aside Schedule orders and BPAs for small business or any socioeconomic subcategory including SDB, and may consider socioeconomic status when deciding which contractors to include in an RFQ. Agencies also claim SDB credit toward their small-business goals for orders placed with SDB Schedule holders, and GSA actively markets small/disadvantaged Schedule holders to buyers ([GSA small business MAS page](https://www.gsa.gov/buying-selling/purchasing-programs/gsa-schedules/small-and-disadvantaged-business-utilization)). Practical effect: SDB status makes Frasier a more attractive quote *recipient* and eligible for set-aside eBuy RFQs; it does not shortcut getting the Schedule. Keep the SDB self-certification current in SAM Reps & Certs. (Note: SDB self-certification thresholds/regime are under periodic policy churn — reconfirm status language at offer time. UNVERIFIED beyond current SAM state.)

### 14. Processing timelines

**Published vs. reality:** GSA's target for new-offer evaluation is ~120 days; industry-reported medians run **6–9 months**, with first-time offerors that draw clarification letters at 12+ months ([Blackfyre timeline analysis](https://www.blackfyre.app/blog/how-long-does-gsa-schedule-approval-take); [GovCon Giants guide](https://govcongiants.com/guides/gsa-schedule)). **FASt Lane offers: as fast as ~45 days**, with 24-hour response expectations during review ([GSA FASt Lane](https://www.gsa.gov/technology/it-contract-vehicles-and-purchasing-programs/multiple-award-schedule-it/fast-lane-making-it-easier); [Winvale](https://info.winvale.com/blog/everything-need-know-gsas-fast-lane-program)). All secondary figures UNVERIFIED against GSA's current internal metrics; treat as planning ranges.

### 15. Current refresh quirks worth knowing

- **Refresh 31 (Apr 2026):** TDR mandatory everywhere; PRC/CSP abolished; Springboard tied to FASt Lane; FAR 15.4 pricing deviation (no support data up front). ([Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/02/goodbye-prc-hello-tdr-breaking-down-gsas-latest-shift-in-refresh-31))
- **Refresh 32 (Jun 2026, current):** reference-verification teeth (invoices/SOWs on demand; rejection of self-owned-entity references); substitutions formally Springboard-only; PPQ/reference alignment required. (SCP-FSS-001 JUN 2026, quoted above.)
- **Refresh 33 (anticipated Sep 2026):** FASt Lane consideration expands beyond IT with "agency sponsorship **or documented customer demand**"; Springboard stays FASt-Lane-gated; TDR enforcement tightens. ([iQuasar advance-notice analysis](https://iquasar.com/blog/gsa-just-dropped-the-refresh-33-advanced-notice-here-is-why-this-one-is-different/)) **Re-check the actual Refresh 33 text before submitting anything** — the offer is evaluated under the refresh in force at submission.
